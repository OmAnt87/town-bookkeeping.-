# Alaska data status

Generated by `node scripts/ak/status.mjs`. 88 governments loaded. Alaska has 19 organized boroughs (listed as counties) and about 145 cities. Much of the state is the Unorganized Borough, which has no borough government. Anchorage, Juneau, Sitka and Wrangell are consolidated city-boroughs. Each borough's file holds the borough and the cities whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Alaska differs
- **Boroughs are listed as counties.** Census areas in the Unorganized Borough have no government of their own and are not listed.
- **Schools are part of borough and some city governments**, so their spending is included and kept out of the services and overhead shares, as in Connecticut.
- **Anchorage, Juneau, Sitka and Wrangell** are consolidated city-boroughs and are shown once, at their borough's center.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Data checks** (`js/engine/validate.js`, run by the app, the builds and `npm test`): 1 not graded until fixed: City of Chefornak (Money in ($182,000) and money out ($1,371,000) are too far apart for a complete report; part of one side is probably missing).
- **Political money** comes from the Alaska Public Offices Commission (`node scripts/ak/fetch-apoc.mjs`): the CSV export of its campaign disclosure income search, one report year at a time. Each report names the municipality and office, so mayor, assembly and city council candidates are placed directly. Some municipalities run their own campaign disclosure, so a borough or city with no APOC reports may still have candidates who raised money.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (3 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
