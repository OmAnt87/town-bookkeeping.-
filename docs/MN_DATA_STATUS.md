# Minnesota data status

Generated by `node scripts/mn/status.mjs`. 2554 governments loaded. Minnesota has 87 counties, about 850 cities and 1,780 townships. Townships govern the areas outside cities, mostly maintaining township roads; cities are separate from the township around them. Counties run human services, public health and county highways. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the governments whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. The Census counts Minnesota's townships as township governments. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Minnesota differs
- **County auditors and treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city and township reports show only their own levy.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.
