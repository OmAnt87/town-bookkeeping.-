# Arizona data status

Generated by `node scripts/az/status.mjs`. 67 governments loaded. Arizona has 15 counties and 91 cities and towns, and no townships. Counties provide city-type services in unincorporated areas, and much of the state is tribal or federal land outside any city. Public schools, fire districts and other special districts are separate governments. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and towns whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Arizona differs
- **Special districts are separate.** Fire districts, water and irrigation districts and others have their own budgets, so some services residents pay for are not in city or county reports.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Data checks** (`js/engine/validate.js`, run by the app, the builds and `npm test`): every record passes.
- **Data check warnings, money in and money out far apart (often a bond-funded project; check the source report):** Graham County, Pinal County.
- **Political money is not scored.** Contributions to Arizona county and city candidates were not loaded.
- **Coverage is about two thirds.** The Census estimated, or has incomplete records for, 39 Arizona governments, including 4 counties and small cities such as Nogales, Bisbee and Globe, so they are not listed. The largest cities are included.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (8 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
