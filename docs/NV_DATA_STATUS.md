# Nevada data status

Generated by `node scripts/nv/status.mjs`. 28 governments loaded. Nevada has 16 counties, Carson City (a consolidated city and county) and 18 other cities. Counties provide most services in unincorporated areas, including the Las Vegas Strip. Each county's file holds the county and the cities whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Nevada differs
- **Counties run large urban areas.** Clark County governs the Las Vegas Strip and much of the metro area outside the cities.
- **Carson City** is a consolidated city and county and is shown once.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money** comes from the Secretary of State's C&E / FD Report Search (`node scripts/nv/fetch-sos.mjs`, which drives a browser with Playwright because the site turns away scripts): each county's and city's candidates by election year, with the office from each candidate's page, and the contribution search's CSV export month by month. The export names the recipient but not the office, so contributions are joined to candidates by name, and a name filed in more than one place is skipped. It has no contributor type either, so businesses, unions and PACs are recognized by name and some are missed.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (2 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
- **Estimated by the Census, not reported (left out, 6):** City of Fallon, Elko County, Esmeralda County, Lander County, Lincoln County, Storey County.
- **Incomplete in the Census files (only debt or a few figures, or spending under a quarter of revenue; left out, 1):** City of Carlin.
- **Older year used** because the latest sampled year was estimated or incomplete: City of Mesquite (FY 2022; FY 2024 estimated), City of North Las Vegas (FY 2023; FY 2024 estimated), City of Sparks (FY 2023; FY 2024 estimated).
- **Transparency practices** have not been checked yet, so they are not scored.
