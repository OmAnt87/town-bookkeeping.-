# Wisconsin data status

Generated by `node scripts/wi/status.mjs`. 1917 governments loaded. Wisconsin has 72 counties, about 190 cities, 420 villages and 1,240 towns. Towns govern the rural areas outside cities and villages, and cities and villages are separate from the town around them, so each resident has one municipality and one county. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the governments whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. The Census counts Wisconsin's towns as township governments. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. Every Wisconsin county, city, village and town reported to the Census in 2022, so coverage is nearly complete.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions and places).

## How Wisconsin differs
- **One bill, several levies.** Municipal treasurers collect the school district, county and technical college levies on the same tax bill, but their Census figures show only their own levy (revenue is close to spending), so the property tax rules used for northern New England are not applied.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money is not scored.** Local candidates file campaign finance reports with their municipal clerk, and contributions to county candidates were not loaded.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (584 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
- **Estimated by the Census, not reported (left out, 0):** none.
- **Incomplete in the Census files (only debt or a few figures, or spending under a quarter of revenue; left out, 4):** GREENVILLE TOWN, Town of Kingston, Town of Pence, Town of Two Creeks.
- **Not in current Census geography (left out):** Town of Madison (the Town of Madison was dissolved into Madison and Fitchburg in 2022).
- **Older year used** because the latest sampled year was estimated or incomplete: none.
- **Transparency practices** have not been checked yet, so they are not scored.
