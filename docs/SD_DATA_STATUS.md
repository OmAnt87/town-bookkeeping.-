# South Dakota data status

Generated by `node scripts/sd/status.mjs`. 463 governments loaded. South Dakota has 66 counties, about 155 cities, 150 towns and about 900 organized townships. Townships govern rural areas outside cities and towns, mainly maintaining township roads. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the governments whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. The Census counts South Dakota's townships as township governments. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions and places).

## How South Dakota differs
- **County treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city, town and township reports show only their own levy.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money is not scored.** Contributions to South Dakota county and local candidates were not loaded.
- **Coverage is about a third.** The Census estimated, or has incomplete records for, about 800 South Dakota governments, mostly townships and small towns, plus 25 counties, so they are not listed.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (18 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
