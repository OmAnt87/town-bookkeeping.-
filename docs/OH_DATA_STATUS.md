# Ohio data status

Generated by `node scripts/oh/status.mjs`. 1156 governments loaded. Ohio has 88 counties, about 250 cities, 680 villages and 1,300 townships. Townships serve the areas outside cities and usually include the villages inside them, so village residents typically pay a village and a township. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the governments whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. The Census counts Ohio's townships as township governments. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. The Auditor of State publishes each government's audit individually, and Ohio Checkbook covers only the local governments that opt in.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions and places).

## How Ohio differs
- **Income taxes.** Most Ohio cities and villages levy a municipal income tax, often their largest revenue; the Census files count it with other local taxes, shown as "Sales and other local taxes".
- **County treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city, village and township reports show only their own levy, and the property tax rules used for northern New England are not needed.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money is not scored.** County and local candidates file campaign finance reports with their county board of elections, not with the state.
- **Coverage is about half.** In 2022 the Census estimated rather than received figures for roughly 1,000 Ohio governments, mostly small townships and villages; Youngstown and 35 counties are among them, so they are not listed.
