# Montana data status

Generated by `node scripts/mt/status.mjs`. 124 governments loaded. Montana has 56 counties and about 130 cities and towns. Anaconda-Deer Lodge and Butte-Silver Bow are consolidated city-county governments. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and towns whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Montana differs
- **Anaconda-Deer Lodge and Butte-Silver Bow** are consolidated city-county governments and are shown once, at their county's center.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Data checks** (`js/engine/validate.js`, run by the app, the builds and `npm test`): every record passes.
- **Data check warnings, money in and money out far apart (often a bond-funded project; check the source report):** Town of Moore, Town of Winifred, Town of Ryegate, Town of Hingham, Petroleum County, City of Poplar, Town of Medicine Lake, Wheatland County, City of Harlowton, City of Judith Gap.
- **Political money** comes from the Commissioner of Political Practices' Campaign Electronic Reporting System (`node scripts/mt/fetch-copp.mjs`): its county and city candidate lists and contribution downloads for each election year, joined by candidate ID. County candidates are placed by their county of residence; city candidates are listed with no city, so they are placed by the city of their mailing address. Montana bars corporate contributions to candidates, so organizational money is mostly committees, and COPP dates many contributions to the first day of their reporting period.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (11 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
