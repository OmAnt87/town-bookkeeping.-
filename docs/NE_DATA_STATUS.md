# Nebraska data status

Generated by `node scripts/ne/status.mjs`. 559 governments loaded. Nebraska has 93 counties, about 150 cities and 380 villages, and township governments in some counties. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and villages whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. Almost every Nebraska city and village reported to the Census in 2022, so municipal coverage is nearly complete.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions and places).

## How Nebraska differs
- **County treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city and village reports show only their own levy.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money is not scored.** Contributions to Nebraska county and local candidates were not loaded.
- **About half the counties are missing:** the Census estimated, or has incomplete records for, 45 Nebraska counties, including Douglas County.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (110 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
- **Left out (333):** Nebraska's township governments (in the counties that have them). Their Census records show only property tax and road spending, with spending about twice the reported revenue, so their revenue appears incomplete.
