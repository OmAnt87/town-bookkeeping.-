# Utah data status

Generated by `node scripts/ut/status.mjs`. 265 governments loaded. Utah has 29 counties and about 250 cities and towns, and no townships. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and towns whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Utah differs
- **Special service districts are separate** (many fire, water and sewer services), so some services residents pay for are not in city or county reports.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps

- **Data check warnings, money in and money out far apart (often a bond-funded project; check the source report):** City of Corinne, Town of Howell, Town of Amalga, City of Castle Dale, Town of Elmo, City of Mona, Town of Garden City, City of Emigration Canyon, Town of Brighton, Town of Francis, City of Erda, Town of Genola, Town of Charleston, Town of Loa.
- **Political money is not scored.** Contributions to Utah local candidates were not loaded.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (12 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
