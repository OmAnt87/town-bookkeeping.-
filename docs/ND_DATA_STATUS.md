# North Dakota data status

Generated by `node scripts/nd/status.mjs`. 908 governments loaded. North Dakota has 53 counties, about 355 cities and about 1,300 organized townships. Townships govern rural areas outside cities, mainly maintaining township roads; unorganized areas are served by the county. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the governments whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. The Census counts North Dakota's townships as township governments. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How North Dakota differs
- **Townships often show surpluses.** Many small townships take in more than they spend in a year, holding road reserves; in the oil-producing west they also receive a share of oil tax revenue.
- **County treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city and township reports show only their own levy.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.
