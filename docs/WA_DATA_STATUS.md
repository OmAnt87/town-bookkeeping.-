# Washington data status

Generated by `node scripts/wa/status.mjs`. 312 governments loaded. Washington has 39 counties and about 280 cities and towns, and no townships. Counties provide city-type services in unincorporated areas. Public schools, ports, fire, library and public utility districts are separate governments. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and towns whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. Every Washington county and almost every city and town reported to the Census in 2022, so coverage is nearly complete.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Washington differs
- **Special districts are separate.** Fire, water, park, library and port districts have their own budgets, so some services residents pay for are not in city or county reports.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money** comes from the Public Disclosure Commission's contributions dataset on data.wa.gov (`node scripts/wa/fetch-pdc.mjs`): business, PAC and union contributions to candidates for mayor, city or town council and city attorney, and for county executive, council or commission, sheriff and other county offices, since three years ago. Receipts are placed by the candidate's jurisdiction. Candidates who raise little can use mini-reporting and itemize nothing, so a government with none listed is not scored. Individuals, candidates' own money and parties are not counted.
- **Eight governments are missing:** the Census estimated their figures.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (40 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.
- **Estimated by the Census, not reported (left out, 8):** City of Granger, City of Kahlotus, City of Mesa, City of Moses Lake, City of Rock Island, City of Winlock, Town of Hatton, Town of Malden.
- **Incomplete in the Census files (only debt or a few figures, or spending under a quarter of revenue; left out, 0):** none.
- **Older year used** because the latest sampled year was estimated or incomplete: Chelan County (FY 2023; FY 2024 estimated), City of Redmond (FY 2022; FY 2024 estimated), City of Bremerton (FY 2023; FY 2024 estimated).
- **Transparency practices** have not been checked yet, so they are not scored.
