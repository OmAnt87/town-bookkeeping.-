# Colorado data status

Generated by `node scripts/co/status.mjs`. 304 governments loaded. Colorado has 64 counties and about 270 cities and towns, and no townships. Counties provide city-type services in unincorporated areas. Public schools and many special districts (metropolitan, fire, water, park and recreation districts) are separate governments. Each county's file holds the county and the cities and towns whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. Almost every Colorado county, city and town reported to the Census in 2022, so coverage is nearly complete.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Colorado differs
- **Denver and Broomfield are consolidated cities and counties** and report as one city each, covering city and county services.
- **Special districts are separate.** Colorado has many metropolitan, fire, water and park districts with their own taxes, so some services residents pay for are not in city or county reports.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Data checks** (`js/engine/validate.js`, run by the app, the builds and `npm test`): every record passes.
- **Data check warnings, money in and money out far apart (often a bond-funded project; check the source report):** 26 governments, including Town of Hooper, Town of Columbine Valley, Town of Campo, Town of Kit Carson, Clear Creek County, Town of Larkspur, Town of Avon, Town of Ramah, Town of Kiowa, Town of Brookside, Town of Pitkin, Town of Lake City, Town of La Veta, Town of Bethune, Town of Bayfield, Town of Berthoud, Montrose County, Town of Naturita, Town of Manzanola, Town of Sawpit, Town of Telluride, Town of Blue River, Town of Erie, Town of Keenesburg, Town of Lochbuie.
- **Political money is scored for counties only**, from the Secretary of State's TRACER bulk files (run `node scripts/co/fetch-tracer.mjs`): itemized cash contributions from businesses, unions and political committees since three years ago to candidates whose committee name shows a county office (commissioner, sheriff, clerk, treasurer, assessor, coroner, surveyor, or "for <County> County"). TRACER gives a committee's county but not its office, and school board candidates file the same way, so committees whose names do not show the office are left out and the totals are a floor. City and town candidates, Denver's and Broomfield's included, file with their municipal clerk and are not scored.
- **Refinancing left out of debt service (17 governments):** principal repaid with new borrowing that was not spent on capital that year is treated as refinancing or a rolled-over note and left out; each affected report says how much.

- **Incomplete in the Census files (only debt or a few figures, or spending under a quarter of revenue; left out, 21):** Town of Aguilar, Town of Branson, Town of Crook, Town of Eaton, Town of Fowler, Town of Hayden, Town of Moffat, Town of Nunn, Town of Orchard City, Town of Otis, Town of Paoli, Town of Parachute, Town of Peetz, Town of Raymer (New Raymer), Town of San Luis, Town of Sedgwick, Town of Starkville, Town of Sugar City, Town of Two Buttes, Town of Ward, Town of Wiley.
