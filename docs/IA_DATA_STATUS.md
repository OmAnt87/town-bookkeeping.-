# Iowa data status

Generated by `node scripts/ia/status.mjs`. 997 governments loaded. Iowa has 99 counties and about 940 cities; every incorporated place is a city, however small. Iowa's townships are not counted as governments by the Census (their trustees mostly look after cemeteries and contract for fire protection), so none are listed. Public schools are run by separate school districts. Each county's file holds the county and the cities whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used. Almost every Iowa city reported to the Census in 2022, so city coverage is nearly complete.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Iowa differs
- **County treasurers collect property tax** for every government, so city reports show only their own levy.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.
