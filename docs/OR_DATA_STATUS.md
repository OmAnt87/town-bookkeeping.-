# Oregon data status

Generated by `node scripts/or/status.mjs`. 190 governments loaded. Oregon has 36 counties and about 240 cities, and no townships. Counties provide city-type services in unincorporated areas. Public schools, fire, water and park districts are separate governments. Each county's file holds the county and the cities whose people mostly live in it.

## Sources
- **Revenue, spending and debt:** what each government reported to the U.S. Census Bureau's [Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/gov-finances.html) (individual unit files), FY 2022-2024, with the same mapping as the Carolinas and Delaware. 2022 is a Census of Governments year covering every government; later years cover a sample, so each government's latest reported year is used.
- **Population:** U.S. Census Bureau 2025 population estimates. **Map location:** Census 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions).

## How Oregon differs
- **Special districts are separate.** Fire, water, park, library and port districts have their own budgets, so some services residents pay for are not in city or county reports.
- **Payments to other governments are left out**, as in the Carolinas and Delaware.

## Known gaps
- **Political money is not scored.** Contributions to Oregon county and city candidates were not loaded.
- **Coverage is about two thirds.** The Census estimated, or has incomplete records for, 86 Oregon governments, including 8 counties and Beaverton, so they are not listed.
