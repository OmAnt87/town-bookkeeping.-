Privacy

No cookies, no accounts, no personal data. Here is everything TownView keeps.

Visit counts

To learn which towns people look up and whether the site is useful, TownView counts visits with GoatCounter, an open-source counter that sets no cookies. A count is one request that holds only:

  • the page, such as /town/city-of-hartford-ct/, and its title;
  • the name of an action, such as share, follow or search-map. What you type into a search box is never sent, only that a search happened;
  • the website that linked you here, on your first page only.

GoatCounter also sees what any website sees: your browser type, screen size and rough location from your IP address. GoatCounter says it turns these into totals and does not store your IP address or build a profile of you. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Nothing is counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or if you turn counting off below.

What stays in your browser

  • Followed towns and your light or dark theme are saved in your browser's storage. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing your site data removes them.
  • A file you load on the Data page stays in your browser until you close the tab.
  • Find your town works out the nearest towns in your browser. A ZIP code you type is used to fetch one small table of ZIP areas from townview.org, named after its first three digits; the full ZIP is never sent. If you use your location, it is read once and never leaves your browser.
  • The last town you viewed is saved so the map can offer to take you back.

Other services

  • Map tiles come from Esri and fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them directly, as with any website that uses them.
  • Town forums run on GitHub Discussions through giscus. They load only when you open a forum, and posting needs a GitHub account under GitHub's own privacy terms.
  • RSS feeds are plain files. Fetching one is not counted.

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
